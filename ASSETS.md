# Asset provenance

Every asset used in this repository, with its origin and licence. Adapted from the
standard the PSoXide SDK already follows
(`psoxide/sdk/crates/psx-font/vendor/PROVENANCE.md`).

This project is **unlicensed non-commercial homebrew** (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)),
so every asset below must be usable *as non-commercial distribution*.

The assets fall into three groups:

1. **Authored here** — the game code, and every model, texture and background
   generated by it. Covered by `LICENSE`.
2. **Vendored open source** — the PSoXide SDK and its fonts and sound effects,
   each with an upstream licence recorded in its own `PROVENANCE.md`.
3. **AI-generated** — **all music and all cinematics**, generated by Google's
   Gemini. These sit under Google's AI-output terms, not a stock-music licence.
   See [Media](#media--all-ai-generated).

There are no rows left with an unknown licence. The one outstanding item is
recording the **plan/tier and generation date** for the AI assets, so the terms in
force at the time can be looked up later.

**If anyone commercialises this project later**, the AI-generated assets must be
re-checked first: a free-tier or trial AI licence is not a commercial one, and
AI-generated output does not attract copyright protection.

### What this does and does not settle

**Settled.** Because the game is distributed non-commercially, Google's
free-tier AI-output terms are compatible with how it is actually released. The
earlier concern — that free-tier output is non-commercial and the artwork claimed
a retail product — no longer applies. Nothing needs re-generating.

**Still needs recording.** Google's AI terms have changed several times, and
whether the output is owned outright, licensed non-exclusively, or subject to a
tier restriction depends on **the plan the files were generated under and the
terms in force at that date**, neither of which is recorded anywhere in this
repository. To close this out:

1. Note the plan/tier and the generation date for each file above.
2. Save a copy of, or link to, the Google terms in force on that date.
3. If any of it was generated on a trial or free tier and is later wanted
   commercially, re-check before relying on it.

**A copyright caveat, stated plainly.** Purely AI-generated output with no
meaningful human authorship is generally **not eligible for copyright
protection** (this is the position taken by the US Copyright Office, and similar
positions exist elsewhere). That affects the *author's* ability to assert an
exclusive right in these tracks — it does not affect the right to distribute
them, and it does not make them someone else's property. It is worth
understanding, though: `LICENSE` retains copyright in the **game code and
artwork**, and the credits roll should not claim authorship of the music.

### Filename typos

`Music/Credts-Below_the_Reeds.mp4` and `Music/Credts/Menu-King_of_the_Yarra.mp4`
both misspell "Credits". Neither is referenced by the build, so they can be renamed
or deleted freely.
