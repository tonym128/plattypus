## 1. WARNINGS & HEALTH PRECAUTIONS
### Photosensitive Seizure Warning
A very small percentage of individuals may experience epileptic seizures when exposed to certain light patterns or flashing lights. If you or anyone in your household has an epileptic condition, consult your physician prior to playing.

### Handling Your Compact Disc
- Do not touch the shiny recording surface of the disc. Always hold it by the outer edge or center hole.
- Clean the disc with a soft, lint-free dry cloth wiping gently in straight lines from the center outward. Never use abrasive cleaners or solvents.
- Store disc in its jewel case when not in use to prevent scratching and exposure to direct sunlight.

## 2. STARTING UP & HARDWARE SETUP
1. Insert the **PLATTYPUS: TACTICAL ESPIONAGE OPERATION** disc into your PlayStation® console with the label side facing upward.
2. Connect a standard Digital Controller or DualShock® Analog Controller to Controller Port 1.
3. Insert a PlayStation® Memory Card into Memory Card Slot 1. *PLATTYPUS requires 1 Memory Card block to save campaign progress, VR simulation badges, and unlocked costumes.*
4. Press the **POWER** button to initiate boot. The animated Burrow HQ title sequence will stream from disc.

Deep within the pristine wetlands of Victoria, Australia, peaceful wildlife habitats are under siege. A rogue corporate syndicate led by the sinister Dr. Cane Toad and his mechanised enforcers has erected illegal industrial encampments across Healesville Sanctuary, polluting the Upper Yarra riverbanks and threatening indigenous species.

His primary mission: infiltrate four high-security sectors, disable tactical searchlight mechs and patrol cutters, recover lost intelligence pages, and safeguard baby sister Pip's nursery burrow before zero hour!

## 5. TACTICAL INFILTRATION MANEUVERS
### Upright Stance vs. Prone Belly-Crawl
* **Standing Infiltration**: Full movement speed. Useful for crossing open pavement quickly.
* **Belly-Crawl (Prone)**: Press **CIRCLE** to drop onto your belly. Reduces Platty's hit profile by 50%, muffles footstep acoustics, and permits crawling beneath low laser barriers, security conduits, and pipe ducts.

### Aquatic Infiltration (Submerged Mode)
Plattypus excels in river waterways! Jump into rapids, mangroves, or surf channels to enter swim mode:
* **Surface Swimming**: Conserves energy and allows rapid river transit.
* **Submerged Diving**: Hold **CROSS** or **SQUARE** underwater to dive below surface searchlights. Watch your O2 gauge! Surface before oxygen depletes to avoid asphyxiation damage.

### Cardboard Box Infiltration
* **Deploy Disguise**: Press **L1** to conceal Platty under an authentic Melbourne Fruit Co cardboard box. Sentry patrols will ignore stationary boxes unless bumped!

## 9. CQC & SUBTERFUGE: THE BILL BOX
### Silent Rear Takedowns (Venom Spurs)
Platty possesses calcaneus venom spurs on his rear ankles. When approaching an unsuspecting sentry from behind, press **CROSS** to execute a Close Quarters Combat (CQC) strike!
* **Silent Rear Strike**: Instantly incapacitates sentries for 15 seconds without triggering an alarm (+500 pts).
* **Frontal Strike**: Causes an 8-second stun but risks alerting adjacent patrol units (+200 pts).

### The Bill Box (Cardboard Disguise)
Press **CIRCLE** to slip beneath "The Bill Box" cardboard shipping container:
* **Motionless**: Completely ignored by sentry vision cones and sweeping searchlights!
* **Moving in Vision**: Shuffling cardboard boxes will arouse immediate guard suspicion ("?!"). Move only when sentries look away!

## 10. ACOUSTIC SURFACE DETECTION GUIDE
Ground surfaces transmit acoustic vibrations based on material density:
* **Metal Grating (High Resonance)**: Generates loud footsteps audible up to 160 units away. Sentry units will investigate immediately!
* **Pavement & Asphalt (Medium Resonance)**: Moderate acoustics. Audible at medium range; crawl to mute.
* **Riverbank Mud, Grass & Water (Low Resonance)**: Dampened acoustic profile. Ideal for covert stalking.
